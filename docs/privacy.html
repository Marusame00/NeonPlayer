Privacy Policy

NeonPlayer

Android application com.neonplayer · Effective 23 November 2026

NeonPlayer does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. It has no internet permission, no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. Everything the app knows about your music stays in its private storage on your device.

What the app accesses

  • Audio files on your device. With your permission, NeonPlayer reads your device's media library to list the music you already have. It reads titles, artists, albums, track numbers, years, durations and embedded cover art. Files are only read, never modified or deleted.
  • Files and folders you pick. If you use "Files" or "Folder", Android's system file picker gives the app read access to exactly the items you choose. That access is remembered so the app can reopen them later, and you can revoke it at any time in Android settings.

What the app stores, and where

All of the following is kept in NeonPlayer's private app storage on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app:

  • Your music library index (titles, artists, albums, file locations, durations).
  • Playlists you create and the current playback queue.
  • Your settings, such as theme, visualizer, equalizer and Stage Mode preferences.
  • The last played track and playback position, so playback can resume.
  • Cached, downsampled album art.

None of this is uploaded anywhere. The app declares no INTERNET permission, so it is technically unable to send data off your device.

Permissions and why they are used

  • READ_MEDIA_AUDIO (Android 13+) or READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 12 and lower): to scan your music library.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+): to show the playback notification and lock-screen controls. Optional; playback works without it.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE and FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK: to keep playing while the app is in the background.
  • WAKE_LOCK: to keep the CPU awake during playback.

NeonPlayer does not request RECORD_AUDIO. Its visualizers read the audio stream inside the player, so the microphone is never used.

Third parties

NeonPlayer contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs, and no third-party service receives data from the app. If you install NeonPlayer from Google Play, Google processes the download and any optional crash or diagnostic data according to Google's Privacy Policy; that is handled by the store, not by the app.

Children

NeonPlayer is a general-purpose music player. It is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Your choices

  • Deny or revoke music access in Android settings. The app then shows an empty library until access is granted again.
  • Remove access to individual files or folders you added, in Android settings under the app's storage permissions.
  • Clear the app's data or uninstall it to erase everything it stored.
